How to translate a Discord server into 22 languages

If your community mixes people from many countries, the hard part isn't translating one message. It's letting everyone just talk, in their own language, without stopping to run a command. This guide shows how CYRON does that.

The problem with translating by command

Most translation bots wait for someone to type /translate or react to a message. That works for the occasional sentence, but in a busy chat nobody does it: the conversation splits by language, and half the server stops reading the other half.

How CYRON works

  1. Each member picks a language in the entry channel. CYRON gives them a role and a chat room in that language, for example #chat-id for Indonesian or #chat-zh for Chinese.
  2. They write in their own room, in their own language. The message appears in every other language room, translated, with the author's name and a small tag like 🇮🇩 → 🇬🇧.
  3. Replies come back the same way. A player in Thailand answers in Thai, and the English room reads it in English.

Stickers, GIF links and emojis cross between rooms too, including stickers and emojis from other servers. Edits and deletions follow the original message.

Other ways to translate

  • Flag reactions: react to any message with a country flag (🇯🇵 🇮🇩 🇧🇷 …) and the translation arrives privately. Free forever, in any channel.
  • Translate button: under an announcement, each person picks a language and gets a private copy.
  • Images and voice: text in screenshots and voice messages can be translated too.
  • Glossary: game terms such as "rally" or "bear" stay as they are instead of being turned into something else.

Who it's for

CYRON was built for gaming alliances in international servers, like the mobile strategy games where one alliance has players from Brazil, Indonesia, China, Thailand, Vietnam and Europe and needs to coordinate rallies and events at the same time. It fits any Discord community whose members speak different languages.

Languages

English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, Vietnamese and Filipino.

Price

Flag-reaction translation and the translate button are free, with no limit. The language rooms, copied channels and image/voice translation are part of the Pro and Alliance plans. While CYRON is in beta, every server gets the Alliance plan for free.

Setting it up

  1. Add CYRON to your server.
  2. Members choose their language; CYRON creates the rooms when someone needs them.
  3. Adjust everything in the ⚙️-cyron channel, or with /cyron.
